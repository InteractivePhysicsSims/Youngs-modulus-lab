Mathematical Formula Guide

1. Tensile Stress ($\sigma$)

Force per unit cross-sectional area applied to the wire.

$$\sigma = \frac{F}{A} \quad [\text{N/m}^2 \text{ or Pa}]$$

2. Tensile Strain ($\varepsilon$)

Fractional extension or ratio of elongation to original length.

$$\varepsilon = \frac{\Delta L}{L_0} \quad [\text{dimensionless}]$$

3. Young's Modulus ($E$)

Measure of stiffness of a solid material.

$$E = \frac{\text{Stress}}{\text{Strain}} = \frac{F \cdot L_0}{A \cdot \Delta L}$$
